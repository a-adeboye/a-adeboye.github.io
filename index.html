Engineering & Security

Building resilient autonomous hardware, optimized energy systems, and secure control networks.

I am a Mechanical Engineering undergraduate at the University of Ibadan specializing in robotics, energy systems, and embedded technology. Recognizing the vital intersection of mechanical engineering and system security, I actively pursue Cybersecurity and Ethical Hacking to build physical devices, sensor networks, and autonomous hardware resilient against structural and digital vulnerabilities.

My technical expertise spans CAD modeling, CFD, FEA, C/C++, Python automation, PCB design, control systems, and cybersecurity analysis. Driven by a commitment to sustainable engineering aligned with UN SDGs, I am an active student member of the Nigerian Institution of Professional Engineers and Scientists (NIPES), ASME, SPE, IMechE, and NSE.
